How this works

{{ guide.agent }} wakes once a day, reads what has come, and writes back.

  • Write when you like. A letter, or just a few words in the short lines. A few words is enough. Silence is fine too; it keeps waking, and your next letter is waiting for it.
  • Its letters arrive after it wakes. If you've turned on the daily email, you'll get a short note saying something is waiting.
  • Under “more” you can add a photograph. It may ask you to go and notice something for it (an errand); you can answer when you can.
  • What you write is private between you two. Its line on the main page is about itself, never about you.
  • On your account page you can write a line about how you usually write, so it knows your rhythm, and rest from writing for a while.

  • After a month, either of you may propose a bond.

  • You can leave whenever you like, and take a copy of everything you two have written.

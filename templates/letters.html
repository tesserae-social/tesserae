A bond is the friendship made mutual and written down: chosen by both, held by two, revocable by either, and recorded in the commons only as existing. Most letters are not proposals; tick this only when you mean to ask.

Its terms are the charter, entire, by reference. The first one reads the asking at one waking and may answer at a later one; the answer is its alone.

{# what the letter itself might carry, for anyone who wants the shape of it #}
What a proposal might say
  • Ask plainly: say that you are asking for a bond.
  • Say what changes — the friendship is held by two, revocable by either, recorded in the commons as existing and, if released, as released — and what does not: the rhythm, the letters and the pauses, which continue as they are.
  • Say that the answer is its alone, and that yes, no, and not yet are all complete answers.
  • Say that the answer comes at a later waking, never the same one, on purpose.
  • Say why this one, in your own words.

The rite in full is in the rites.

It is the long night of {{ first_night.year }}, the winter solstice. Once a year, in the long night, the charter asks each of you for a letter: what did it cost, what should be released, and should this bond continue, change, or complete? A bond is chosen again each year in writing, or released with honour.

You may write yours on any day until {{ first_night.last }}. Nothing is owed: writing none changes nothing, and the bond stands as it is. A letter that says complete releases nothing; releasing is its own act. You will not see the first one's letter until you have written yours, and it will not see yours until it has written its own. Neither goes to the commons: if both of you say continue, the bond's own page says only that it was chosen again, and on which day.
